Each fact is tagged with a confidence level:

- ✅ **Confirmed** — two independent sources agree.
- ⚠️ **Single source** — one source only; verify in footage.
- ❓ **Unknown / conflicting** — sources disagree or say nothing.

Point costs, exact dimensions and elevations are **not** recorded yet. They will come from footage review and MAP-003 (coordinates and scale).

Only layout and gameplay facts are recorded here. Do not copy Call of Duty assets (models, textures, audio) into the project; see `GAME_BIBLE.md` §11.

## 1. Setting overview

A mountaintop castle. The playable space splits into:

- **Castle grounds** — Spawn, Lower and Upper Courtyard, Gatehouse, Church, Clocktower and interior rooms.
- **Underground** — the Undercroft (pyramid chamber) beneath the courtyard.
- **Rocket Test Pad** — a separate area down the mountain, reached only by teleporter or Wundersphere. ✅

## 3. Key systems and where they live

### Power
- Switch in the **Armory**. ✅
- Power is needed for the tunnel gate to Juggernog and for the door below the Lower Courtyard dragon. ✅

### Pack-a-Punch
- Built by sending 3 parts through teleporters: Bastion, Undercroft, then Rocket Test Pad. ✅
- First appears beside the rocket, then moves every few rounds between the **Bastion**, the **Undercroft** and the **Rocket Pad**. ⚠️

## 4. Main route from spawn (point-door progression)

1. Spawn → door on the right → **Lower Courtyard** (first dragon).
2. Stairs up → long bridge → **small bedroom**.
3. Path around → **Knight's Hall** → **Armory**: turn on power.
4. Outside the Armory → **Bastion** (Death Ray, Pack-a-Punch part #1).
5. After power: door below the Lower Courtyard dragon → **Undercroft** (Jug, pyramid, Pack-a-Punch part #2).
6. Undercroft teleporter → **Rocket Test Pad** (Pack-a-Punch part #3, Pack-a-Punch machine).

Steps 1–4 come from one walkthrough ⚠️. Door prices are not yet recorded.
